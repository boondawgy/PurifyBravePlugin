PLEX → PURIFIER

Purify for Plex

Send a movie to your Purifier queue from its three-dot menu.

Used when its server ID matches the selected movie. Other servers are discovered through your Plex account.

Stored locally in this extension. Sent only to Plex. Find your token.

File path mapping

Default: /Volumes/Media/Media/Movies/Film.mp4 → /media/Movies/Film.mp4. Prefix matching is case sensitive. Paths outside this prefix are rejected.

Purifier must have a saved profile named Movie. Requests contain file_path, profile, and title.
